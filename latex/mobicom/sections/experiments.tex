% ============================================
% Section: Experimental Evaluation
% ============================================
\label{sec:experiments}

We evaluate Co-LIC2 along five axes: (1) odometry accuracy against single-node
and multi-agent baselines, (2) rendering quality of the merged Gaussian map,
(3) bandwidth consumption and end-to-end latency, (4) scalability under
increasing node count, and (5) robustness under injected link failures and node
churn. Unless otherwise noted, all experiments use the default configuration
parameters listed in Table~\ref{tab:config}. \textcolor{red}{Red-colored values
in tables denote estimates and placeholder targets pending ongoing hardware
evaluation on physical multi-rig deployments.}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}

\paragraph{Hardware.} Each node runs on a commodity edge platform: NVIDIA Jetson
Orin AGX (32 GB, 2048-core Ampere GPU, 12-core ARM Cortex-A78AE CPU) with a
dual-band Wi-Fi~6/6E NIC (Intel AX210, 2$\times$2 MIMO) or a Qualcomm X65 5G
NR modem for cellular-mode experiments. The optional Tier-0 server runs on a
desktop workstation (Intel Core i9-13900K, NVIDIA RTX~4090, 64 GB RAM). All
nodes are time-synchronized to $<$1~ms via PTP (IEEE 1588) for ground-truth
comparison, though Co-LIC2 does not rely on this synchronization.

\paragraph{Datasets.} We evaluate on three categories of sequences:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Single-node LIV datasets} (for backward-compatibility
    verification): FAST-LIVO2~\cite{zheng2024fastlivo2_mob} (outdoor
    multi-camera + multi-LiDAR), M2DGR~\cite{yin2022m2dgr} (ground robot with
    LiDAR + stereo + IMU), and MCD~\cite{nguyen2024mcd} (urban driving).
  \item \textbf{Multi-agent public datasets}: the INS multi-agent
    dataset~\cite{deng2025mneslam} (3--5 RGB-D agents, indoor) and the
    GRAND-SLAM sequences~\cite{thomas2025grandslam_mob} (2--4 monocular agents,
    indoor/outdoor).
  \item \textbf{Custom multi-rig recordings}: \textcolor{red}{two-to-four
    physical rigs (an SUV with 2$\times$ Hesai PandarXT + 4$\times$ stereo
    cameras, a UGV with 1$\times$ Ouster OS1 + 2$\times$ fisheye, and 1--2
    quadrotors with Realsense D455 + Garmin LiDAR-Lite v3) covering 0.5--2.0 km
    trajectories on a university campus. These sequences are in preparation;
    preliminary timestamps and odometry baselines exist, but full multi-rig
    Co-LIC2 results are pending.}
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Baselines.} We compare against:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Single-node}: Gaussian-LIC2~\cite{lang2026gaussian2_mob} (the
    direct ancestor), FAST-LIVO2~\cite{zheng2024fastlivo2_mob} (multi-LiDAR,
    multi-camera LIV odometry), and GS-LIVM~\cite{xie2025gslivm} (LIV Gaussian
    mapping).
  \item \textbf{Multi-agent}: CGS-SLAM~\cite{deambrogi2026cgsslam_mob},
    MNE-SLAM~\cite{deng2025mneslam} (neural implicit), and a naive
    \emph{pose-graph-only} baseline where per-node Gaussian-LIC2 maps are
    aligned via GNSS priors without our registration and merge modules.
  \item \textbf{Protocol ablation}: Co-LIC2 with the QoS scheduler disabled
    (all traffic mapped to AC\_BE, no deadline-awareness), and with the NPV
    compressor replaced by a naive fixed-SH-degree=2 compressor.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Metrics.} Odometry: Absolute Trajectory Error (ATE) and Relative Pose
Error (RPE) at 5~m/10~m intervals~\cite{zhang2018tum}. Rendering: PSNR, SSIM,
and LPIPS (AlexNet) on held-out evaluation frames rendered at 720p from the
merged global map. Bandwidth: per-node uplink throughput, cluster-head
ingress/egress, and application-layer packet delivery ratio (PDR). Latency:
end-to-end $T_{\text{e2e}}$ (submap boundary to merged-map availability).
Scalability: per-link bandwidth and head CPU/GPU utilization vs.\@ node count.

\subsection{Odometry Accuracy}

We first verify that Co-LIC2's per-node odometry matches Gaussian-LIC2 on
single-node datasets (backward compatibility, goal G7), then evaluate the
multi-agent scenario where inter-node constraints improve odometry.

\paragraph{Single-node compatibility.} Table~\ref{tab:odom_single} reports ATE
on three standard sequences. Co-LIC2 with cooperative mode disabled achieves
identical accuracy to Gaussian-LIC2, confirming that the generalized
multi-sensor front-end introduces no regression.

\subsection{Ablation Study}

We ablate three key design choices:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{QoS scheduler disabled} (all streams on AC\_BE, no deadline
    awareness).
  \item \textbf{NPV compressor replaced} by a fixed-degree=2, fixed-quantize
    =2 cm compressor without budget feedback.
  \item \textbf{Confidence weighting disabled} in conflict resolution (simple
    averaging instead).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{table}[t]
  \centering
  \caption{Ablation results (4 nodes, INS indoor). \textcolor{red}{Red = placeholder.}}
  \label{tab:ablation}
  \footnotesize
  \setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lcccc@{}}
    \toprule
    Variant & ATE (m) & PSNR & BW (Mbps) & $T_{\text{e2e}}$ (ms) \\
    \midrule
    \textbf{Full Co-LIC2}  & \textcolor{red}{\textbf{0.11}} & \textcolor{red}{\textbf{29.7}} & \textcolor{red}{3.0} & \textcolor{red}{\textbf{1280}} \\
    No QoS sched.\@        & \textcolor{red}{0.13} & \textcolor{red}{29.5} & \textcolor{red}{3.0} & \textcolor{red}{2450} \\
    Fixed SH=2 compr.\@    & \textcolor{red}{0.11} & \textcolor{red}{\textbf{29.7}} & \textcolor{red}{5.2} & \textcolor{red}{2180} \\
    No conf.\@ weighting   & \textcolor{red}{0.15} & \textcolor{red}{28.9} & \textcolor{red}{3.0} & \textcolor{red}{1350} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textcolor{red}{Key ablations: (1) Disabling QoS scheduling nearly doubles
$T_{\text{e2e}}$ (1.28$\to$2.45 s) because pose and submap streams compete for
AC\_BE, causing head-of-line blocking. (2) The fixed compressor uses 73\% more
bandwidth without improving PSNR, validating the NPV-guided budget controller's
efficiency. (3) Removing confidence weighting degrades ATE by 36\% because
low-quality drone Gaussians pollute the map during merge.}
